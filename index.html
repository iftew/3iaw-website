Fly Race
System

A multiplayer flying race minigame you can drop into any game. Players line up, get a flying tool and race through rings in the sky. The fastest flyers win.

  • Auto queue: step on the green pad, and a countdown starts once 2 or more players are waiting
  • Flying tool: double click on PC or double tap on phone to fly, and it's taken away after the race
  • 20 sky rings: fly through them in order before the 50 second timer runs out
  • Rings only in races: racers see them with a marker on the next ring, and they're hidden otherwise
  • Top 3 winners: first, second and third place are announced when the race ends
  • Saved wins: wins are saved, and the top players show on a global in game leaderboard
  • Win reward: the winner gets 100 of your leaderstats currency (optional, amount and name changeable)
  • Anti cheat: a speed check catches players moving faster than flying allows
Perfect for
HangoutMinigamesObbyEvents

Polished UI with a queue counter, countdowns, a race timer, live progress and ring sounds. Phone friendly: works with the joystick, and players look up or down to climb or dive. One config file sets players needed, countdown, race time, rings, winners and the tool name.

Kite Fighting
System

Fly kites high over your map, tangle your string with a rival's, bet on it and race to cut their line. Inspired by Shakrain, the kite festival of Old Dhaka, and built for hangout and festival games.

  • Floaty kite physics: lag, drift and sway, so it flies like a kite, not a drone
  • Big sky: climb up to 110 studs with up to 95 studs of line, plus a kite tail
  • Readable crowds: every flyer gets their own color, with a live flight readout
  • PC and mobile: on screen steering pad and line buttons, clear of jump and the thumbstick
  • Real tangles: strings tangle only when they actually cross in 3D, not on touch events
  • Bet duels: both players name a bet, raise or MATCH, then a 3 second countdown and a 5 second TAP! race
  • Winner takes the pot: the loser's kite is cut with a 15 second cooldown, and a tie refunds both
  • Server safe: bets held by the server, balances rechecked, an anti autoclicker cap, and leaving forfeits
Perfect for

Setup: 3 scripts and a Tool, with every value tunable in one config. Bets and winnings use your game's existing leaderstats currency.

Donation
System

A complete Pls Donate style system with real Robux transfers, not gamepass tricks. Players send Robux straight to each other, Roblox shows the payment dialog, and your game never touches the money.

  • Real Robux transfers: built on Roblox's official Robux Transfers API, and Roblox handles the payment
  • Donation bowl: every player (or only your group) gets one, with quick picks or any custom amount
  • No surprises: the panel shows Roblox's 10% fee and the limits up front
  • 5 effect tiers: Donation, Generous, Divine, Mythic and Legendary, plus an effect on the receiver
  • Server wide hype: gold chat announcement, on screen banner and a chime
  • Leaderboards: global Top Donors and Top Raised, 50 entries each, auto refreshing
  • Podium statues: wearing the #1 donor's and #1 raiser's real avatars, plus a TOP DONOR tag
  • Drops right in: DataStore saves, joins your leaderstats, and a /simdonate test command
Perfect for

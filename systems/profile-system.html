Profile
System

Give every player their own profile. Click or tap any player to see who they are, leave a comment, and show some love.

  • Click to view: tap any player (or yourself) to see their avatar, display name and @username
  • Bio: write your own bio (150 characters), filtered and saved
  • Likes and dislikes: vote on other players' profiles, with live counts
  • Comments: a chat message, a sound and a beam linking the two players for every comment
  • Pin and hide: pin up to 3 favorite comments to the top, and hide ones you don't like
  • Add friend and View avatar: send a friend request or see a player's full outfit right from the profile
  • Hide player: hide annoying players (their avatar, chat and voice) just for you
  • Saved forever: profiles, comments and likes are saved with DataStores

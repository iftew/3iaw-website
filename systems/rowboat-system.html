Rowboat
System

Calm, good looking rowboats for lakes and rivers. Hop in, row and drift. Perfect for hangouts, parks and roleplay maps.

  • Floats properly: rides Terrain water at the right height, so the hull never looks swamped
  • Feels like a boat: gentle bobbing and rocking, leaning into turns, and the bow lifts with speed
  • Rowing pace: calm, not speedboat fast, with a beaching guard that keeps boats off the shore
  • Rowing animation: the driver's stroke speeds up with the boat
  • Real oars: a mirrored pair that pivots at the oarlocks and dips into the water on each stroke
  • Water sounds: lapping and bow wash that fade in with speed
  • Two seats: one for the driver and one for a passenger
  • Every control: WASD or arrow keys, gamepad, and built in on screen controls for mobile

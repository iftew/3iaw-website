Partner
System

Bring anyone into your game as your partner. Type any Roblox username and a partner shows up wearing that player's real avatar, even if they're offline.

  • Call anyone: type any Roblox username to get a partner with their exact avatar, clothes and accessories, even if they're offline
  • Spawn and Recall: the partner appears beside you with a sparkle effect, and Recall brings them back anytime
  • Follow: your partner walks with you and matches your speed, on or off whenever you like
  • 4 carry styles: Piggyback, Bridal Carry, Hug and Carry, picked from the menu
  • Bye Bye: send your partner away with one tap
  • Clean dark UI: a smooth panel with pop up messages, opened from a Call Partner topbar button
  • Works in any game: carry poses are built in, so there's nothing to upload, and every player sees them
  • Safe: usernames are checked on the server, lookups have a cooldown, and everything is cleaned up when a player leaves

Phone friendly, and carry styles, positions, follow distance, walk speed and the button are all in one config file. Carry needs R15 avatars.

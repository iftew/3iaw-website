// 03 — Tutorial

How to use the converter

From an audio file to a sound playing in your Roblox game.

Video coming soon
Step by step
  1. Log in with DiscordYour account gets 2 free uploads. A plan gives unlimited uploads.
  2. Connect RobloxCreate an Open Cloud API key with Assets read and write, and paste it in Roblox account.
  3. Choose your audioUse audio you made or have permission to use, or a track from Roblox's own audio library.
  4. Pick a presetDefault keeps the sound as it is. Adjust Speed, Amplify and Max Duration if you need to.
  5. Convert & uploadThe audio goes to your account and Roblox reviews it. Follow the status in History.
  6. Use it in your gameWhen it's approved, copy the link or ID and set it as a Sound's SoundId in Roblox Studio. Then set PlaybackSpeed for your preset (Default: 0.43). History lists the value for every preset.

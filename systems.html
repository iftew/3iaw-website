Fly Race
System

A multiplayer flying race minigame you can drop into any game. Players line up, get a flying tool and race through rings in the sky. The fastest flyers win.

  • Auto queue: step on the green pad, and a countdown starts once 2 or more players are waiting
  • Flying tool: double click on PC or double tap on phone to fly, and it's taken away after the race
  • 20 sky rings: fly through them in order before the 50 second timer runs out
  • Rings only in races: racers see them with a marker on the next ring, and they're hidden otherwise
  • Top 3 winners: first, second and third place are announced when the race ends
  • Saved wins: wins are saved, and the top players show on a global in game leaderboard
  • Win reward: the winner gets 100 of your leaderstats currency (optional, amount and name changeable)
  • Anti cheat: a speed check catches players moving faster than flying allows
